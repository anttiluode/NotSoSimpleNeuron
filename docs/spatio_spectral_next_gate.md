# Next gate: spatio-spectral distributed routes

The next experiment combines the mechanisms that survived separately.

A route will own sparse contacts across several short branches. Different branches will have different local temporal dynamics. Route identity will therefore be expressed by both **where** its contacts land and **when** its pings arrive.

The central comparison will be against a matched bank of point/vector resonators with the same state budget. Morphology only earns the next step if the structured branch bank provides useful separation, reuse, robustness, or parameter efficiency that the matched attacker does not.

The first version should avoid learning, inhibition, soma adaptation, AIS structure, and growth. Those remain later gates. This keeps the experiment focused on one question:

> Does coupling sparse spatial address to heterogeneous receiver timescales buy anything beyond an equally sized non-spatial dynamical bank?
